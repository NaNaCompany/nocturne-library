# Rights and provenance

Verified 2026-10-26. This collection selects English originals whose principal authors died by 1946. It assesses the underlying text for the United States and Korea, and separately records the license of the Standard Ebooks edition work. It makes no blanket claim that every original is public domain in every country.

## Underlying original texts

Each official Standard Ebooks repository's LICENSE.md states that its source text is believed to be in the United States public domain. Those exact statements are retained in `exports/sources.json`, with a commit-specific license URL for every edition. The most recent original in this set was first published in 1925; no modern copyrighted translation is used.

The [Korea Copyright Commission's explanation of copyright duration](https://www.copyright.or.kr/information-materials/common-sense/basic-knowledge/index.do?jspName=08) gives the general rule of the author's life plus 70 years, calculated from January 1 of the following year. All selected principal authors died in 1946 or earlier. Even applying the full 70-year period conservatively to all of them, it ended by December 31, 2016. Earlier expiry under historical transitional rules is not needed for this conservative conclusion.

The author's death year is independently sourced to an official Library of Congress record or finding aid, rather than inferred from a filename. The following table records the reasoning; it is not a claim of worldwide expiry. Original historical quotations and prefaces already included in these editions remain part of the historical text. Mary Shelley's historical preface contributions by Percy Bysshe Shelley (died 1822), for example, do not introduce a modern translator.

## Standard Ebooks edition contributions

Every selected repository's own LICENSE.md dedicates the creators' and contributors' edition contributions to the worldwide public domain under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 permits copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use. The dedication of edition contributions and the expiry of the underlying original are separate parts of the reuse rationale. The legal instrument is [CC0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode).

No trademark endorsement or affiliation is asserted. Standard Ebooks is credited as the edition source, and its complete role-based contributor credits and original transcription/scan source links are retained in `exports/sources.json`. Attribution is supplied for provenance even though CC0 does not require a copyright attribution condition. Cover artists and font designers may occur in the original metadata credits; this does not mean their image/font assets were exported. Only text is used.

## Modification and extraction notice

The source is extracted from XHTML to UTF-8 plain text and JSON paragraphs. XML/HTML markup, source indentation, stylesheet information, drawings, alt text, publisher apparatus and backlink arrows are removed. Textual table cells are separated by tabs and poetry retains explicit line breaks. Navigation titles for headingless files and endnotes are derived from source markup. Original wording is otherwise retained; no machine translation or AI rewriting is performed.

The only textual source section intentionally excluded beyond publisher/illustration apparatus is the 52-note set of added English translations of French/German passages in *Jane Eyre*, together with its numeric reference markers. The original French/German quotations and the author's 1848 edition note are retained. Original or historical source notes in *Frankenstein*, *Moby Dick* and *The Time Machine* remain. These include historical explanatory/editorial content from the supplied public-domain textual sources; they are not newly authored translations.

For exact inclusion/exclusion and checksums, use the spine lists, per-file extraction audits, SHA256 manifests and pinned source commits in `exports/sources.json`. The absence of artwork is intentional; the collection should be labeled as text editions. See REPORT.md for the precise textual scope and verification boundary.
